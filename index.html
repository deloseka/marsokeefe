Hello there! ☆

I'm Mars (any pronouns), a graphic designer and character illustrator with a passion for emotionally driven and character focused visual storytelling.

I am currently a freelance artist, with previous experience at my university's press as a social media manager and book cover designer, creating digital assets, promotional material, and layouts to clearly show information in a creative way. I thrive in taking on any visual projects, from editorial design and branding, to character illustration and web design.

Outside of my work, I am always exploring new mediums, such as digital painting, watercolor, web development, and lately, repurposing old technology! My creative curiosity is what drives me.

I have my Bachelors of Arts in Art with a specialization in digital media and design, and a minor in Anthropology. I plan on pursuing my MFA in Digital Media sometime in the future, and my long-term goal is to work as a graphic designer and/or character illustrator that lets me combine storytelling, design systems, and collaborative creative work. Working with others is what makes art so fun for me!

Thank you for reading, and thank you for visiting my website!
